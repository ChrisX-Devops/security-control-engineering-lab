## Three Things People Should Associate With Me

### 1. Honest scope

I claim what I can prove. I do not overclaim OT
capability. This builds trust faster than broad claims
ever could.

### 2. Control systems thinking

I structure security systems the way industrial
controllers structure process control. Continuous
measurement. Defined setpoints. Prioritized alarms.
Closed loops.

### 3. Working systems on GitHub

Every claim I make has a corresponding repository.
Every capability has a demo. The portfolio is the proof.

## What This Brand Position Does

### It differentiates

Most cloud security engineers do not understand
control systems thinking. Most OT security engineers
do not understand modern cloud automation. I sit at
a useful intersection, honestly.

### It builds trust

Honest scope statements ("I can do X, I am building
toward Y, I do not yet do Z") generate more trust
than confident claims of universal capability.

### It opens specific markets

Manufacturing companies, fintechs with hybrid environments,
industrial SaaS platforms — these clients value someone
who understands both worlds without overclaiming either.

### It protects me from bad engagements

If a prospect needs OT pentesting or live SCADA work,
they will move on quickly. That is correct outcome.
Taking work I cannot deliver damages everyone.
