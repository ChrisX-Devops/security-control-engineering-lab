# Objection Handling — Memorize Responses

---

## "We already use Vanta / Drata / Secureframe"

**Bad response:** "Mine is better."

**Good response:**
"Those platforms are excellent for evidence storage and
workflow management. They are not built to validate your
actual cloud configuration in real time.

I have seen companies on Vanta still fail audits because
nobody caught a public S3 bucket or an admin user without
MFA. My system catches the gaps those platforms miss.

We can run side-by-side. Most clients keep both."

---

## "We have a security person already"

**Bad response:** "Fire them and hire me."

**Good response:**
"Perfect. Your security person can use what I build to
focus on higher-value work instead of manual evidence
collection.

A senior security engineer costs about $180k/year. They
should be designing controls, not running queries. I
build the systems that free them up."

---

## "We are not ready for SOC2 yet"

**Bad response:** "You should be."

**Good response:**
"Then this is the perfect time. The companies that struggle
with SOC2 are the ones who start preparing 30 days before
the audit. The companies that breeze through are the ones
who set up monitoring 6 months early.

If we set up continuous compliance now, when you start
the audit you will already have 6 months of clean evidence
ready. No scramble."

---

## "How is this different from a penetration test?"

**Bad response:** "Pentests are pointless."

**Good response:**
"Pentests find vulnerabilities at a single point in time.
What I build is more like a smoke detector — it runs 24/7
and tells you when something changes.

You need both. The pentest finds things attackers might
exploit. My system makes sure your team does not
accidentally introduce new gaps between pentests."

---

## "What if we just use AWS Security Hub?"

**Bad response:** "Security Hub is incomplete."

**Good response:**
"Security Hub is great for AWS-native findings. It does
not connect those findings to your SOC2 controls or
generate auditor-ready evidence.

I take the technical findings from tools like Security
Hub and translate them into the evidence and reporting
format your auditor actually wants. Different layer of
the stack."

---

## "We do not have budget right now"

**Bad response:** "When will you?"

**Good response:**
"What is the cost of failing your next audit?

Most failed audits delay enterprise contracts by 3-6
months. I have seen single deals worth more than 10x
my annual fee.

Can I send you a one-page ROI calculator?"

---

## "Can you guarantee we will pass our audit?"

**Bad response:** "Yes."

**Good response:**
"No one can guarantee an audit outcome — be careful of
anyone who does. What I guarantee is that you will know
exactly where you stand before the audit starts.

If we run my assessment and you address the critical
findings, you eliminate 80%+ of the issues that cause
audit failures. The remaining variables are documentation
and operational evidence, which I can also help with."

---

## The Universal Reframe

When you do not have a perfect answer, use this:

"That is a fair question. Let me ask you this: if you
had a magic wand, what would your ideal compliance
process look like?"

Then listen. They will tell you exactly what to sell them.
