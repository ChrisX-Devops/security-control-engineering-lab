# 30-Second Pitch — Memorize This

---

## Version 1: For Founders and Non-Technical People

"I help fintech and SaaS startups pass SOC2 audits without the
80-hour evidence collection nightmare.

Most founders I work with spend weeks of engineering time
manually collecting evidence, then get stuck in months of
back-and-forth with auditors.

I built an automated system that connects to their cloud,
validates security controls, and generates the exact evidence
auditors need. Most clients reduce their audit prep time by
60% and avoid the kind of failures that happen when someone
forgets to enable MFA on an admin account.

If your company is preparing for an audit or already in one,
I can run a free 30-minute scan and show you exactly where
you stand."

---

## Version 2: For Technical People (Engineers, CTOs)

"I build security control engineering systems. Specifically
automated pipelines that:

- Extract cloud state via AWS APIs
- Validate against SOC2 baselines using OPA/Rego
- Detect behavioral anomalies in audit logs with SQL
- Generate evidence for compliance frameworks

It is what Vanta and Drata do conceptually but actually
validates your environment instead of just storing your
manual evidence uploads.

I have a portfolio of working systems on GitHub. Want me
to send you the link?"

---

## Version 3: For LinkedIn DMs (Cold Outreach)

"Hi [Name], saw your post about [their challenge].

Quick question: how is [Company] handling SOC2 evidence
collection right now? Most teams I work with end up doing
it manually which eats 80+ hours per audit cycle.

I built a system that automates this. Happy to send a quick
demo video if useful."

---

## Practice Method

1. Read each version aloud 5 times
2. Record yourself on your phone
3. Listen to the playback
4. Identify where you stumble
5. Rewrite that part in simpler language
6. Repeat until you can deliver each version smoothly in 30 seconds

The goal is not memorization. The goal is fluency. You should be
able to adapt these to any conversation naturally.
